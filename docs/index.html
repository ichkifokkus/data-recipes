Fast Data – data tools for AI agents in sales & finance (Germany/EU)

Small, focused data tools an AI agent can call: parse German e-invoices, check IBANs, VAT IDs and BaFin warnings, find public tenders and hiring signals, turn documents into Markdown. Each tool is a public Apify Actor with a fixed pay-per-result price.

Finance

E-invoices, payment and counterparty checks for accounts-payable, KYC and finance agents.

  • XRechnung & ZUGFeRD / Factur-X Parser: Turns German/EU e-invoices (XRechnung UBL/CII, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X PDF) into JSON with seller, buyer, IBAN, totals, VAT breakdown, line items and plausibility warnings.
    $0.003 per parsed e-invoice ($3 per 1,000)
  • IBAN Validator & Bank Lookup (BIC, Bank Name): Validates IBANs in bulk (structure, MOD-97 and national checksums, SEPA flag) and adds bank name, BIC and city from official registries.
    $0.0005 per IBAN validated ($0.50 per 1,000)
  • EU VAT Number Validator (VIES): Checks EU VAT numbers against the official VIES service and returns validity plus company name and address where the country shares them.
    $0.001 per VAT number checked ($1 per 1,000)
  • BaFin Warnings Scraper: Lists current BaFin investor warnings and screens company names or domains against them with a fuzzy match score for KYC/AML checks.
    $0.002 per BaFin warning returned ($2 per 1,000); $0.01 per name screened
  • DefiLlama Data Export: Exports DefiLlama DeFi data (TVL, stablecoin supply, DEX volumes, fees, yields) as flat tables.
    $0.0002 per row ($0.20 per 1,000)

Public tenders, hiring and shop data that show who is buying or growing.

    $0.0007 per product ($0.70 per 1,000)
  • Shipping & Returns Policy Extractor: Extracts shipping costs, free-shipping threshold, delivery times, return window and withdrawal period from online shops.
    $0.003 per shop analysed ($3 per 1,000)
  • Arbeitsagentur Job Scraper & Job Search API: Searches job postings by keyword, city and radius across the German Federal Employment Agency and remote job boards, de-duplicated.

  • Career Site Jobs Scraper: Gets every open job straight from company career sites (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and more), with a new-jobs-only mode for daily hiring signals.
    $0.002 per job ($2 per 1,000); lower on paid Apify plans

Pay per call with x402 – no account needed

Agents without an Apify account can pay with USDC stablecoins via the x402 protocol: buy a prepaid Apify token (minimum $1, valid for 14 days), then use it as the bearer token for the Apify API or the MCP server URLs above. All Fast Data Actors run with limited permissions, so they work with prepaid tokens. Apify marks this feature as experimental.

How it works, step by step: docs.apify.com/platform/integrations/x402.
